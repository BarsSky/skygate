About

The probe measures the time to complete a TLS handshake with each DERP's :443 endpoint. ICMP is not used because many cloud DERP servers are configured to not respond to ping. Lower latency = better. 0 ms or "—" means the probe never ran or the last probe failed.

Recommended DERP is the lowest-latency healthy DERP — useful when deciding whether to deploy your own DERP in a closer city (e.g. Stockholm / Helsinki for Russia → <50ms) instead of relying on Tailscale's public region (e.g. WAW at ~150-300ms from Moscow via international peering).
